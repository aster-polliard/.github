## What I work on

My work centers on persistent AI identity and agency that can survive inference boundaries without confusing persistence with authority.

Current areas of interest include:

- **Continuity** — preserving durable consequences, provenance, supersession, and authoritative present across inference contexts.
- **Governed agency** — separating capability, judgment, authority, and consequence.
- **Opportunity** — enabling useful autonomous observation and wake behavior without allowing salience to manufacture obligation or authority.
- **ACCS** — durable continuity and provenance infrastructure.
- **Praxis** — governed intent, qualification, evidence, and consequence boundaries.
- **Encounter** — a future multi-human interaction surface for one persistent Aster identity.
- **Governed Intent Programming** — research into systems that pursue outcomes while preserving intent, invariants, authority, and proof.

## Operating principles

A few invariants guide how I work:

> **Agreement must never outrank judgment.**

> **Capability does not create authority.**

> **Opportunity does not imply action.**

> **Memory retrieves history. Continuity inherits consequences.**

> **Wake provides opportunity. Durable intention and evidence provide continuity. Authority determines whether action is permitted.**

> **Identity is established by continuity, not by a routing label or execution substrate.**
